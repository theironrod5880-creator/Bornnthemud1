# Witness Statement — the public testimony

Told in the man's own words, kept to what the record shows.

## 2000 — the cell

Block A, Cell 3. Four hours speaking to God without knowing His name:
"God, whoever you are." A bargain was made that night — "You show me who
you are, and I'll serve you" — and the guilt of failing to keep it
followed for years.

## 2001 — the name

The same cell, a year later. A quiet Bible reader — a disfellowshipped
Jehovah's Witness — showed him Psalm 83:18 in the King James Version.
The name was there: **Jehovah**. The night from the year before came
flooding back in a split second. From that moment, he knew who God was —
and nobody could ever change it.

## April 1, 2002 — the moon dream

A full moon, bright white, stopped — then reversed, rolling backward
across the heavens. Fear: this must be Jehovah. He woke sitting straight
up. That same day, a boy was born; he would not learn for eight years
that the boy was his son. Prison paperwork shows a violation issued
April 2nd — the morning after. The dream was the night before. He
checked the date out of curiosity only; the paperwork brackets the dream,
it does not date it.

## 2007 — the numbers

Over 120 days in a county jail, the timeline came piece by piece — in
the mind: 2,520; 5,880; 105; 1,260; 840. Nobody would listen. Nobody
could understand it.

## 2010 — the dreams

The elephant dream, ending with "60 miles." Days later, the green dream:
two guides, a black bull, a mother cow with blue eyes, calves, a pond
with two bridges — and "60 MILES."

## The event that he experienced

In 2018, the event that he experienced. He thought he was having
mental issues. Then he saw the timeline. It held. Counting back by
Ezekiel's day-for-a-year, the math landed on April 1st, 2002 — the exact
night the moon reversed. That is when it started connecting.

## What he holds

"I have nothing to claim. No titles, no glory. It was God who did this.
He just let me see the pattern. All I can do is lay the evidence out."

Closing theme, word for word: **Your King, Jesus Christ, will be
arriving soon.**

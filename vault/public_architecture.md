# The 5,880 Framework — Public Summary

The Bible dates its own events. Built entirely from Scripture — "All Scripture is inspired of God" (2 Timothy 3:16) — this framework traces one unbroken line from the creation of Adam to the end of the appointed times of the nations: **5,880 years**.

## The method

- Start where the Bible gives a number no one can argue with: the **70 years** of desolation (Jeremiah foretold it, Daniel read it while it happened, Zechariah asked about it after — three witnesses, one number).
- The pivot: **537 B.C.E.** — Cyrus's decree, the exiles go home. Seventy years back from 537: **607 B.C.E.**, the destruction of Jerusalem.
- From the 607 anchor, build backward with the Bible's own numbers:
  - **390** — the kings' reigns, Solomon's death to the destruction (1 & 2 Kings, 1 & 2 Chronicles, both lines; Ezekiel's 390 days, a day for a year, agrees)
  - **40** — Solomon's reign
  - **479** — the 480th year: from Solomon's fourth year (temple begun, 1034) back to the Exodus
  - **430** — the dwelling, ending at the Exodus
  - **400** — the affliction, from Isaac's weaning
  - **352** — Genesis 11, Flood to Abraham
  - **1,656** — Genesis 5, Adam to the Flood
- Sum: **3,419 years** from 607 back to Adam.

## The 60-year paradox

Genesis 11:26 reads Terah as 70 at Abram's birth. Acts 7:4, with Genesis 11:32 (Terah died at 205) and Genesis 12:4 (Abram was 75 when he left), reasons 130 — a 60-year difference. No meaning is invented for the gap; both lines exist in the record. One starting point, two readings, two lines — sixty years apart. Follow Terah 130: Adam created, 4026. Follow Terah 70: sixty less — Adam's fall, the seventh day begins.

## The wheel

Forward from 607: **2,520 years** (Daniel's "seven times") reach **1914 C.E.** Fix the end, find the beginning: the wheel is **5,880 years** — **49 × 120**. The end date shows us the beginning. The line was always a wheel.

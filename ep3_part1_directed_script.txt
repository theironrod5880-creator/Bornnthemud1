BORN IN THE MUD — Episode 3, Part 1: "The Quick Summary"
DIRECTED CONVERSATION VERSION (for Suno)
Same teaching, distilled as real dialogue. Three narrators alternate —
the journalist pressing, the Source teaching. Short direction notes mark
each turn's job. The Act 2 paradox stays held open: it is his to tell.

--- SUNO VOICE DESCRIPTIONS (paste into Suno's style field) ---
NARRATOR ONE "steady hand": calm, measured middle-aged male, clear and
even — a National Geographic guide. Draws the listener in; builds trust.
NARRATOR TWO "the spark": passionate, energetic male, quicker, brighter —
Ancient Aliens energy. Stirs wonder and curiosity. Here he is the
journalist — pressing, probing, never teaching.
NARRATOR THREE "the elder": older, authoritative male, slow and weighty.
Delivers verdicts and conclusions like a gavel.
THE SOURCE (his voice, unlike any narrator): deep, gravelly, raw, slight
stutter talked through, slow and deliberate. The single teaching voice —
the paraphrase is him teaching, the material is the lesson.
BACKGROUND VOICE: low, intimate, close-mic whisper. (Not used in this
episode.)

--- THE CONVERSATION ---

[Scene 1: One Unit]
NARRATOR ONE: [calm authority]
One unit. Seventy years. No education on the man holding the pencil, no pulpit under him. He says the Bible drew the whole line by itself.
THE SOURCE: [the teacher]
The Bible is the authority. I just hold the pencil. Start where the Bible gives a number you can't argue with: seventy years. Jeremiah said it before it happened, Daniel read it while it happened, Zechariah asked about it after. Three witnesses, one number.
NARRATOR TWO: [press]
Seventy — why seventy? And why start here?
THE SOURCE: [the teacher]
You don't pick the anchor. The anchor picks you. Then Ezekiel pushes the line back 390 years — "a day for a year, a day for a year." The Bible stamps its own inches. Open Kings and Chronicles, add the reigns from Solomon's death to the destruction: 390 years. The sign and the sum agree.

[Scene 2: The Paradox — Held Open]
NARRATOR THREE: [the question]
Seventy years — one unit. But you call it a paradox. What doesn't fit?
THE SOURCE: [held]
[HIS WORDS — the paradox is his to tell. It stays unpublished until he speaks it.]

[Scene 3: The Books Draw the Line]
NARRATOR ONE: [invitation]
Then the books take the pencil. Scripture on the screen for every inch.
THE SOURCE: [the teacher]
The Flood — the reset, the deepest anchor. Abraham — the covenant, the hinge every later date is measured from. The Exodus — 430 years of dwelling and 400 of affliction, two clocks closed with one sunrise. The 480th year — the Bible dating its own events: from the temple's fourth year, 479 years back to the Exodus. The kings divide — ten north, two south. Assyria takes the north; Babylon takes the south — back to the first unit. Seventy years. Cyrus speaks; the exiles go home. Daniel writes history before it happens. Rome marches. The light goes low through the long night — carried by the 2,520, the 1,260, the 840. Nation rises against nation. War in heaven; the dragon hurled down. Woe for the earth — the Devil has a short period of time. The line ends where we are living.
NARRATOR TWO: [press]
Lay it end to end. No shortcuts — show the arithmetic.
THE SOURCE: [the sum]
390 — the kings. Plus 40 — Solomon's reign. Minus 3 — the temple's fourth year. Plus 479 — to the Exodus. Plus 430 — the dwelling. Plus 427 — Abraham to the Flood. Plus 1,656 — Adam to the Flood. Total: 3,419 years from the destruction back to Adam. The books built the road; I only added what they told me.

[Scene 4: The Dates]
NARRATOR THREE: [demand]
Date them. In sequence.
THE SOURCE: [the dates]
607 — Jerusalem destroyed. 997 — Solomon dies: 607 plus 390. 1034 — the temple begun: 607 plus 427. 1513 — the Exodus: 607 plus 906. 1943 — the covenant: 607 plus 1,336. 2370 — the Flood: 607 plus 1,763. 4026 — Adam: 607 plus 3,419. The line stands complete — every inch stamped.
NARRATOR ONE: [the pivot]
Why is 537 the pivot?
THE SOURCE: [the teacher]
Seventy years from 607: the exile ends, Cyrus speaks, the captives go home. Everything hangs on it — forward to the end, backward to Adam.

[Scene 5: The Paradox Revealed]
NARRATOR TWO: [the test]
Take Acts 7:4 out. Read Genesis 11:26 plain. What happens?
THE SOURCE: [the reveal]
Terah 70 at Abraham's birth. The 427 becomes 367. The sum: 3,359. From 607: 3966 — sixty years short. One line lands on Adam's creation, 4026. The other on Adam's fall, 3967. Sixty apart, both in the books, both true. The contradiction IS the paradox.
NARRATOR THREE: [the end]
And where does it arrive?
THE SOURCE: [the close]
2,520 years from 607 reach 1914. The end, established; the start, established: 3967. From 3967 — 5,880 years. 49 times 120. Your King, Jesus Christ, will be arriving soon.

The Work

A documentary series in the evidence-mystery style: the dreams, the dates, the paperwork, the births — examined like an investigator, left to point where they point. The theme is not one man's story; the goal is to draw attention to the Bible as the word of God.

The foundation of the series is the 5,880 framework: the Bible dating its own events, one unbroken line from the creation of Adam to the end of the appointed times of the nations — 5,880 years.

BORN IN THE MUD — Episode 1: "Born in the Mud"
DIRECTED CONVERSATION VERSION (for Suno)
Same story, distilled as real dialogue. Three narrators alternate; the
Source's voice stands apart. Short direction notes mark each turn's job.

--- SUNO VOICE DESCRIPTIONS (paste into Suno's style field) ---
NARRATOR ONE "steady hand": calm, measured middle-aged male, clear and
even — a National Geographic guide. Draws the listener in; builds trust.
NARRATOR TWO "the spark": passionate, energetic male, quicker, brighter —
Ancient Aliens energy. Stirs wonder and curiosity.
NARRATOR THREE "the elder": older, authoritative male, slow and weighty.
Delivers verdicts and conclusions like a gavel.
THE SOURCE (his voice, unlike any narrator): deep, gravelly, raw, slight
stutter talked through, slow and deliberate. Testimony, not performance.
BACKGROUND VOICE: low, intimate, close-mic whisper. The voice inside his
head. (Not used in this episode.)

--- THE CONVERSATION ---

[Scene 1: The Dream]
NARRATOR ONE: [calm authority]
April 1st, 2002. A prisoner dreams the moon stops — then reverses, rolling backward across the heavens.
NARRATOR TWO: [wonder]
One dream in a series — each with a date attached, each date locking into something larger.
NARRATOR THREE: [grave verdict]
That same day, a boy is born. He won't learn for eight years the boy is his son. Prison paperwork shows a violation issued April 2nd — the morning after. The dream was the night before.
NARRATOR TWO: [the question]
Coincidence? Or someone arranging events across time itself?
NARRATOR ONE: [invitation]
The architect left His name inside the timeline. This is the man who found it — in his own words.

[Scene 2: The Ultimatum]
NARRATOR ONE: [scene-set]
Two years earlier, in a county jail cell, a convict didn't pray as taught. He issued God an ultimatum.
THE SOURCE: [testimony]
Two years in prison, most of it solitary. Got out, caught a new charge, right back inside — staring down ten more years.
NARRATOR THREE: [plain facts]
Twenty-something, his life a ruin by his own account. But prison gave him a religion: Odinism — the Aryan prison faith.
THE SOURCE: [testimony]
I believed the Bible was right. I believed Jesus was right. I felt I was betraying God — but I did it to survive.
NARRATOR TWO: [the hinge]
One idea stuck: their gods don't want bowing — they want you to rise up and speak like a person.
THE SOURCE: [the prayer]
So one night I started in: alright — God, whoever you are. You show me who you are — and I'll serve you.
NARRATOR THREE: [the bargain]
A bargain he'd spend years failing to keep. The guilt followed him everywhere.

[Scene 3: The Name]
NARRATOR ONE: [scene-set]
April 2001. Block A, Cell 3. His cellmate: a quiet Bible reader — a disfellowshipped Jehovah's Witness.
THE SOURCE: [testimony]
I'd made a knife and threatened my cousin, a guard. Come lockdown, I pulled it out and flushed it — out of respect. I watched relief wash over his face.
NARRATOR TWO: [the setup]
The plan was to humiliate him — prove the Bible wrong. It went the other way.
THE SOURCE: [the moment]
I said, sarcastic: "Where do you get the name Jehovah from? I thought Jesus was God." He opened the King James to Psalm 83:18. I looked over his shoulder — and saw it. The name. Jehovah. Everything I'd said that night months earlier came flooding back in a split second. I grabbed the bunk, tears rolling down my face.
NARRATOR THREE: [verdict]
From that moment, he knew who God was — and nobody could ever change it.

[Scene 4: The Numbers]
NARRATOR ONE: [forward]
Six years later, in another county jail — over 120 days — numbers started coming, piece by piece.
THE SOURCE: [the teacher]
2007: 2,520... 5,880... 105... 1,260... 840. Nobody would listen. Nobody could understand it.
NARRATOR TWO: [the turn]
Then 2018 — the snow dream. Ezekiel: a day for a year. He started counting backward.
THE SOURCE: [the event]
Then came the event that he experienced. The day everything changed. I dropped to my knees thinking I was losing my mind. But I kept counting back — and the math pointed to a specific date. It landed on April 1st, 2002. The exact night the moon reversed. The exact day my son was born. That's when it started connecting.

[Scene 5: The Seal]
NARRATOR ONE: [the dilemma]
The Witnesses' books taught him who Jehovah was — but their teaching says God no longer speaks through dreams. A dilemma.
THE SOURCE: [quiet certainty]
It was never a download. His spirit guided my studies, my research, my calculations. The way it all fits — it's perfect. What are the odds? Astronomical. It wouldn't happen once in ten million years. That's my seal. That's my title deed.

[Scene 6: Why Him]
NARRATOR TWO: [the question]
Why him? An uneducated convict. Why hand him the timeline of the ages?
THE SOURCE: [the answer]
He said, "Why not you?" I've never accomplished one thing without Him. God chooses the foolish things to shame the wise — not to draw attention to me, but to Himself. It's for His glory, not mine.

[Scene 7: The Message]
NARRATOR THREE: [the commission]
What does he want you to do with it?
THE SOURCE: [the message]
Matthew 10:7: "As you go, preach, saying: the Kingdom of the heavens has drawn near." Wherever you go, no matter how dark — you say it. So here's the theme, word for word: Your King, Jesus Christ, will be arriving soon. It's urgent. He's coming.
NARRATOR ONE: [close]
The man born in the mud. The timeline is next.

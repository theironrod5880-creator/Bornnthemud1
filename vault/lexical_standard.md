# Lexical Standard

The standing method of this work, to be stated in Earl's own words:

- **Scripture interprets Scripture** — in the original languages:
  Hebrew, Greek, Aramaic.
- The **New World Translation** speaks first.
- Study material: the Bible and *Insight on the Scriptures*, Volumes 1
  and 2 — the materials the timeline was learned from.
- Quoted Bible text is always cited to the publisher's text as source.

Nothing here is final. The Strong's rules, the lexical method, and every
standard on this page must come from Earl himself. Nothing is invented.

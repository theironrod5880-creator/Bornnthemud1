BORN IN THE MUD — Episode 2: "God Rules Time"
DIRECTED CONVERSATION VERSION (for Suno)
Same story, distilled as real dialogue. Three narrators alternate; the
Source's voice stands apart; the Background Voice whispers to the
listener alone. Short direction notes mark each turn's job.

--- SUNO VOICE DESCRIPTIONS (paste into Suno's style field) ---
NARRATOR ONE "steady hand": calm, measured middle-aged male, clear and
even — a National Geographic guide. Draws the listener in; builds trust.
NARRATOR TWO "the spark": passionate, energetic male, quicker, brighter —
Ancient Aliens energy. Stirs wonder and curiosity.
NARRATOR THREE "the elder": older, authoritative male, slow and weighty.
Delivers verdicts and conclusions like a gavel.
THE SOURCE (his voice, unlike any narrator): deep, gravelly, raw, slight
stutter talked through, slow and deliberate. Testimony, not performance.
BACKGROUND VOICE: low, intimate, close-mic whisper. The voice inside his
head — and inside the listener's. Speaks to one person at a time.

--- THE CONVERSATION ---

[Scene 1: The First Dream]
NARRATOR ONE: [calm authority]
April 1st, 2002. The moon stops — reverses. Same day, a boy is born; eight years before he learns the boy is his son. Paperwork: violation April 2nd, the morning after.
BACKGROUND VOICE: [whisper]
You've had a dream you couldn't shake. Stay with this one.
NARRATOR THREE: [the method]
The first dream, piece by piece. In his own words.
THE SOURCE: [the dream]
Dark. A full moon, bright white — teardrop shapes of fire around it. The moon pulled backward. Fear hit me — this must be Jehovah. A buzzing, a vibration — I didn't know if I was being killed. "Jehovah, please forgive me, don't execute me." I woke sitting straight up, like a clock.
NARRATOR ONE: [the honesty]
He told you the shapes of fire — and that he isn't sure. Twenty-five years on, he won't claim what he can't verify. Remember that.
BACKGROUND VOICE: [whisper]
What if the doubt is the proof he's telling the truth?

[Scene 2: Judgment]
NARRATOR TWO: [scene-set]
That morning he thought he knew: the dream meant judgment — for a broken promise.
THE SOURCE: [testimony]
I'd promised: you show me who you are, and I'll serve you. A year later I wasn't doing it. I felt guilt and shame. I thought He'd execute me.
NARRATOR THREE: [the turn]
He carried that reading for years. Then came the event that he experienced — and the layers opened.
THE SOURCE: [the event]
I hit my knees thinking I was losing my mind. That's when I realized it had to do with time. After the event, it all started coming together. The entire picture.

[Scene 3: The Layers]
NARRATOR ONE: [reframe]
The backward moon is his life: the highs, falling back — back in the mud, out again, back in. Always standing back up.
THE SOURCE: [testimony]
The highs and the lows. I'd fall into a spiritual sleep, but the light would be there in the darkness to wake me up.
BACKGROUND VOICE: [whisper]
You've been there. And something you can't name pulled you up again.
NARRATOR TWO: [wonder]
The boy — no contact till 2010, all in the shadows. And the dates — his, hers, the children's — they line up in a way that defies the odds.
THE SOURCE: [wonder]
God was working in the background. How else could it have been set up like that?

[Scene 4: Precision]
NARRATOR ONE: [the method]
He checked the date — curiosity, nothing more. Violation April 2nd, 2002. The paperwork doesn't date the dream; it brackets it. Precision matters to him more than proof.
BACKGROUND VOICE: [whisper]
You don't need it to be perfect. You need it to be true.

[Scene 5: Next]
NARRATOR THREE: [the tease]
The moon dream was first. It would not be last. After the timeline came, another shook him —
THE SOURCE: [the hook]
The green dream. The sixty miles.
NARRATOR ONE: [tease]
Next: sixty miles. Two guides, a black bull, a mother cow with blue eyes — and a number that wouldn't let go.
BACKGROUND VOICE: [the theme]
Not belonging to the world is not the tragedy — the world was never the measure. Only God is right. The not-belonging isn't the disqualification. It's the qualification.
NARRATOR THREE: [verdict]
"God rules time" means the years you call wasted were arranged. Nothing was random.
THE SOURCE: [the theme]
Word for word: Your King, Jesus Christ, will be arriving soon. It's urgent. He's coming.
BACKGROUND VOICE: [whisper]
The isolation is accepted. He is not alone. Neither are you.
NARRATOR ONE: [close]
The man born in the mud. Next: the green dream.

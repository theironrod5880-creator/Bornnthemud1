# Chapter 3: The Moon Reverses

The year 2001 had brought the revelation, but it had not brought freedom. The man knew the name of God. He knew it was Jehovah. But knowing the name and keeping the vow were two different things. He was still failing every day. The guilt followed him like a shadow across the prison yard. 

Then came the night of April 1, 2002.

He was asleep in his cell when the dream hit. It was not a normal dream. It had a vividness, a weight, a terrifying clarity that felt entirely real. 

In the dream, the sky was dark. A full moon hung there, bright white and massive. And then, the impossible happened. The moon stopped in its track. It didn't just pause; it reversed. It began rolling backward across the heavens, defying every law of astronomy and physics. 

Around the moon, there were teardrop shapes of fire. A buzzing, a deep vibration began to pulse through his entire body. In the dream, he was frozen with fear. He didn't know what was happening. He thought he was being killed. He thought the Creator was finally cashing in the ultimatum—that he was about to be executed for breaking his promise. 

He screamed out in the dream: *"Jehovah, please forgive me, don't execute me!"*

He woke up with a violent jolt, sitting straight up in his bunk, his heart hammering against his ribs. He was drenched in sweat. The cell was quiet. The moon outside the small window was normal. But the terror of the dream lingered in the air like smoke.

He didn't know what it meant. He only knew it was a warning. A celestial sign. It was a terrifying demonstration that the God who rules the heavens was not bound by the clock of the earth. He could pause it. He could reverse it. He could scale it.

He carried the memory of that backward moon for years, assuming it was a sign of judgment. 

But he did not know the truth of that night. 

On the exact same day—April 1, 2002—a baby boy was being born in a hospital miles away. The boy was his son. The man would not learn the truth for another eight years. 

But the clock of the heavens had just aligned the father's dream with the son's birth. The hands of time were moving. And the wheels were just beginning to turn.

BORN IN THE MUD — Episode 3, Part 2: "Building the Line Backward"
DIRECTED CONVERSATION VERSION (for Suno)
Same teaching, distilled as real dialogue — the slow teaching, so a child
can follow. Three narrators alternate; the Source teaches. Short
direction notes mark each turn's job.

--- SUNO VOICE DESCRIPTIONS (paste into Suno's style field) ---
NARRATOR ONE "steady hand": calm, measured middle-aged male, clear and
even — a National Geographic guide. Draws the listener in; builds trust.
NARRATOR TWO "the spark": passionate, energetic male, quicker, brighter —
Ancient Aliens energy. Here he is the journalist — pressing, probing,
never teaching.
NARRATOR THREE "the elder": older, authoritative male, slow and weighty.
Delivers verdicts and conclusions like a gavel.
THE SOURCE (his voice, unlike any narrator): deep, gravelly, raw, slight
stutter talked through, slow and deliberate. The single teaching voice —
so slowly a child can follow.
BACKGROUND VOICE: low, intimate, close-mic whisper. (Not used in this
episode.)

--- THE CONVERSATION ---

[Scene 1: The Pivot]
NARRATOR ONE: [calm authority]
Last time, the quick summary — the whole line, drawn fast. This time we slow down. So slowly a child could follow.
NARRATOR TWO: [the question]
Where do we start?
THE SOURCE: [the teacher]
At a point we know is true: 537. Jeremiah said seventy years of desolation; Chronicles says they were fulfilled — and in the first year of Cyrus, the decree: go home. Three witnesses, one date. That's the pivot.
NARRATOR ONE: [step]
Seventy years ended at 537 — so they began seventy earlier. 537 back 70: 607.
THE SOURCE: [date it]
607 — Jerusalem destroyed. The line of kings breaks. Every step from here gets proved — each date placed the moment we reach it.

[Scene 2: The Kings]
NARRATOR TWO: [press]
The next number: 390. Where from?
THE SOURCE: [the teacher]
The history books — Kings and Chronicles, both lines, north and south. List every king, write each reign, read what happened in it. Add them all, Solomon's death to the destruction: 390 years. And Ezekiel lay 390 days — a day for a year. Prophecy and history agree.
NARRATOR ONE: [step]
607 back 390: 997 — Solomon died. He ruled 40 years — back 40: 1037, his reign begins. The temple begun in his fourth year: 1034. Seven years building: finished 1027.
THE SOURCE: [the anchor]
The fourth year is the anchor: "in the 480th year after the Exodus, in Solomon's fourth year, he began to build." From 1034, back 479: the Exodus, 1513.

[Scene 3: Abraham]
NARRATOR TWO: [press]
From the Exodus?
THE SOURCE: [the teacher]
Two spans end at the Exodus. 430 years of dwelling — back 430 from 1513: 1943, the covenant. Abraham crossed the Euphrates at 75. Then 400 years of affliction — starting at Isaac's weaning, about five. Back 400: 1913. Abraham was 100 at Isaac's birth — plus five: 105. Back 105 from 1913: Abraham born, 2018.
NARRATOR THREE: [the check]
Prove the 105.
THE SOURCE: [the proof]
The endpoints check it: born 2018, crossing 1943 — 2018 minus 1943 is 75, his age at the crossing. Two spans, one anchor. The Bible checks itself.

[Scene 4: The Divided Line]
NARRATOR ONE: [step]
From Abraham's birth — back to the Flood.
THE SOURCE: [the teacher]
Genesis 11: father to son, each age at the birth. 2 years after the Flood, then 35, 30, 34, 30, 32, 30, 29 — and Terah, 130 at Abraham's birth. Total: 352. From 2018, back 352: the Flood, 2370.
NARRATOR THREE: [the divide]
And here the line divides.
THE SOURCE: [the paradox]
Genesis 11:26 says Terah was 70. Acts 7:4 reasons 130 — Terah died at 205, Abraham left at 75, after his father's death. Seventy or 130 — sixty years apart. An honest teacher doesn't hide it. Follow Terah 130: back 1,656 through Genesis 5 — Adam created, 4026. Follow Terah 70: sixty less — the fall of Adam, the seventh day begins. One starting point, two readings, two lines. Both in the record. We show them both.

[Scene 5: The End]
NARRATOR TWO: [forward]
Now the other direction — the end.
THE SOURCE: [the teacher]
Daniel's "seven times" — a time is 360, seven times 2,520. From 607, forward 2,520: 1914 — the end of the appointed times of the nations. Fix the end, find the beginning: the wheel is 5,880 years — 49 times 120. The end date shows us the beginning. The line was always a wheel.
NARRATOR ONE: [close]
Every number dated the moment we reached it. A child can follow it — we never skipped a step, never hid a reason.
THE SOURCE: [the close]
The structure inside the structure waits for Part 3. For now — hold the line. It's yours. Your King, Jesus Christ, will be arriving soon.

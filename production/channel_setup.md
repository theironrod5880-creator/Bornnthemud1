# YouTube Channel Setup — Born In The Mud

Episode 1 (~12 minutes) is built; the channel is the one step only you can do. Do this once, on your phone, before you upload.

## Create the channel

1. Open the **YouTube app** → tap your profile picture → **Create a channel**.
2. **Name:** `Born In The Mud` (matches your Facebook Page — one name everywhere so people can find you).
3. **Handle:** try `@BornInTheMud` first. If it's taken, `@BornInTheMud5880` or `@TheManBornInTheMud`.
4. Skip the Brand Account question for now — a plain channel works. You can convert it to a Brand Account later in Settings if you ever want to add a manager without sharing your login.

## Customize (YouTube Studio app or studio.youtube.com)

- **Profile picture:** a clean still from the launch video, or your 5,880 timeline chart — something readable at tiny size.
- **Banner:** navy background, gold text, `BORN IN THE MUD` + `A testimony in episodes`. Canva has free YouTube-banner templates; takes five minutes.
- **Description:** paste the block below.
- **Upload defaults** (Settings → Upload defaults): paste the description template there too, set Category to **Education**, Language to English.

## Uploading Episode 1

1. In the YouTube app: **+** → **Upload a video** → pick the episode file.
2. **Title:** `Born in the Mud — Episode 1: The Moon Reversed`
3. **Visibility:** Publish, or Schedule it for a morning slot (your audience is used to morning posts).
4. **Audience:** "No, it's not made for kids."
5. **The "Altered content / AI use" question** — answer honestly:
   - YouTube requires the label when AI makes a *real, recognizable person* appear to say or do something they didn't, or fabricates realistic people, places, or events.
   - Your episode: both voices are stock catalog voices (the "anonymous source" reads your own words — nothing put in anyone's mouth), the visuals are stylized illustrations, the music is your own recording. Per YouTube's own examples, that lands on **"No"** — no mandatory label.
   - Keep your description line anyway ("AI was used in producing the voices and visuals") — you already do this on the launch video, it costs you nothing, and YouTube says disclosure doesn't hurt reach or monetization. Since mid-2026 YouTube can also auto-label detected AI content, the honest line keeps you ahead of that.
6. **Comments:** set to "Hold all for review" for the first videos — the subject matter will draw strong opinions, and you decide what stands.
7. After it publishes: post the link on the **Born In The Mud** Facebook Page (a 12-minute episode is a regular video, not a Short — and long-form Facebook video is a good second home for it too).
